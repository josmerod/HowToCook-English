## Operation

<!-- Describe the complete cooking workflow step by step. -->
<!-- Avoid vague quantities such as "appropriate amount" or "a little". -->
<!-- Unless specified otherwise, ingredient names refer to the full prepared amount. -->

- Peel the potatoes and cut into chunks no larger than 4cm. Set aside.
- Chop the curry blocks into small pieces to help them melt faster. Set aside.
- Heat the pot, add 10-15ml of cooking oil, and wait 10 seconds for the oil to heat up.
- Add the potatoes and stir-fry until *softened* (check by piercing with chopsticks or a fork). <!-- Do not introduce ingredients in the steps that were not listed above. -->
- Add enough water to submerge all ingredients. Bring to a boil, reduce to low heat, and **simmer for 15-20 minutes**.
- Turn off the heat, add the chopped curry blocks, and stir until completely melted. <!-- Waiting steps must specify a duration or a clear visual cue for completion. -->
- Turn the heat back to low and **stir slowly for 10 minutes** to prevent scorching.
- Once the sauce *thickens to a smooth consistency*, turn off the heat and serve.

## Additional Content

<!-- Add extra tips, references, or safety notes here. -->

- Monitor the water level while simmering; if it drops below 2/3 of the ingredients, add hot water until covered.
